## 1. Edit: shell

- `openEdit(id)` is a bottom sheet (`#sheet`, `.efoot` bar at the bottom). It opens from the page's Edit button, a card's pencil, Settings jumps and the tour. Unknown id = nothing opens. First lines (5370-5372): a game that has a played record but `state !== "played"` is silently set to `played` (**[W]**, repairs data).
- `editing` = a deep copy of the game (`JSON.parse(JSON.stringify(g))`). Every control writes to `editing` only; nothing touches `S` until Save. Cancel/close throws `editing` away. Android: keep one `EditDraft` object, same rule.
- Header: the title `g.title` and three tab chips in the order **Game | Look | Scores** (the code lists panels Game, Scores, Look but the chips are Game, Look, Scores). A tab tap scrolls the sheet to the top, repaints the Look preview (`paintPrev`), plays `beep("tap")`.
- Groups (`.setgroup`) are closed `details` rows: bold name + a grey summary (`h4[data-sum]`, strings below). **Exactly one is open at a time**; opening one hides every other group and the tab chips and shows "‹ Back to all" above its name. The plan says 16 groups; the code has **15**: Game 6 (Name, Release, Where it stands, Platforms, Genres and tags, Notes), Scores 4 (Hype, Critics, My rating, Played, and Played only exists when the game has a played record), Look 5 (Art from SteamGridDB and the stores, Cover and colour, Backdrop, Hub page figure, Logo / character art). Without the figure Android has 14 (13 on a game that isn't played). The 16th does not exist in this version: tell the owner, do not invent one.
- Summaries (`editSums`): Name = first 40 chars of "Why you want it" or "No note"; Release = "6 Nov · 20h · confirmed" (date short, hours with h, certainty; "No date" when empty); Where it stands = the state label; Platforms/Genres = `sumList` (the names, or "None picked"); Notes = "learn + tips" or "Empty"; Hype = "7/10" or "—/10"; Critics = "73/100" or "None yet"; My rating = "3 of 4 rated · 62/100" (web quirk: `rated` counts the categories with a score out of the 8, but the text always says "of 4"; the number after is `avgScore(g) x 10` rounded; copy the text as printed, or write "3 of 8" and tell the owner) or "Not rated"; Played = "7.5/10"; Cover = Uploaded / Linked / Placeholder; Backdrop = Off / Set / Auto (`g.bgOff` / `g.hero||g.bg||g.steamId`); Logo = "Set, right|left" or "None".
- Footer (`.efoot`, always visible): **Save**, **Review changes**, **Cancel**, **Remove** (red), a diff area `#e-diff` and a message line `#e-msg`. All four buttons come (owner, Nov 10).

## 2. Game tab

**Name.** Title (`#e-title`, text; blank on save falls back to the old title), "Why you want it" (`g.desc`, one line), "What it's about" (`g.about`, 4 rows, placeholder "A line or two on the story. The RAWG pull fills this in.").

**Release.**
- Release date `#e-date`: a NATIVE date input, value `YYYY-MM-DD` (`g.date`, "" = none), plus a "✕" (`#e-datex`, title "Clear the date"). Android: a Material date picker dialog returning the same string; a clear button. Under it a line (`#e-datesay`): `shortDate(v) + " " + year` (en-GB, "6 Nov 2026") or **"No date — shows as TBA"**. `shortDate` (9223): "d MMM" plus a 2-digit year when not the current year.
- Rules between date and certainty: picking a date while certainty = tba sets certainty `confirmed`; clearing the date (✕ or empty) sets `tba`; tapping `tba` while a date exists clears the date and says **"Date cleared — TBA means there isn't one."** in `#e-msg`. On save: no date -> `status = "tba"`; a date with `tba` -> `confirmed`; else the chosen one (`confirmed | rumoured | tba`, default `confirmed`).
- Date certainty chips `#e-status`: `confirmed`, `rumoured`, `tba` (lowercase words as shown).
- Hours (inputs, numbers, min 0): **Main story** `#e-hours` -> `g.hours`; **Story + extras** `#e-hoursx` -> `g.hoursX` (placeholder "≈ N" = round(hours x 1.5)); **Everything (100%)** `#e-hours100` -> `g.hours100` (placeholder "≈ N" = round(hoursX x 1.6) or round(hours x 2.4)). Empty = 0 = "not set" (a typical estimate is used by `hrsOf`, 9246). No range clamp in Edit (`hrsOf` clamps 1000 / 3000 / 5000 when reading).
- Play style: NOT an Edit control. It is the app-wide `S.playStyle` (`main|extras|all`, Settings, 6484). Edit only prints under the hours: "You play **main story**, so that number is the one used for evenings and plans. Leave the others blank and a typical estimate is used." (lowercase label of the style). The plan's "play style" in Edit = this note; the switch itself belongs to Settings (read-only on Android; show the note from the backup's value).
- Hint lines under the hours: if the game has `g.hbV` (filled by Nexi) and the typed value equals it: "Typical time, filled in by Nexi" (name from `pnName()`; 20582). If `g.hl` (HowLongToBeat) exists: "⏱ HowLongToBeat: **m · x · c h**" and either " · these are them" or a chip **Use these** that copies the three numbers into the inputs (blank when 0). Both are display-only until Save.
- On Save a click listener (23427) then compares the three hours with the old ones: changed -> `g.hrsSrc = "me"` (or `"hltb"` when they equal HowLongToBeat's) and `delete g.hbV` **[W]**.

**Where it stands** (`#e-state`): five chips from `STATES` (3498): Upcoming `upcoming`, Out, not played `backlog`, Playing now `playing`, Played it `played`, Started and dropped `dropped`. Default `upcoming`. On save `syncPlayed(g, state)` (3552): choosing `played` with no record creates `{id, real: 0, t: today}` (and Nexi stamps the page 150ms later); leaving `played` deletes the record **[W]**. Choosing `dropped` (it wasn't) makes Nexi react ("dropped").

**Platforms** (`platPicker`): nine toggle chips with a logo, in this order: PS5, PS4, Switch 2, Switch, PC, Xbox, Steam, iOS, Android. `g.platforms` = a comma list ("PS5, PC"); a tap adds/removes the name at the END of the list; names the picker doesn't know (RAWG's "Wii", "PlayStation 4"...) stay in the string untouched and show as text elsewhere; matching is case-insensitive. Android: keep the raw string, toggle by name, never rewrite the unknown ones.

**Genres and tags** (`genrePicker`): three headed groups of toggle chips, same comma list `g.genres`.
- Kind: JRPG, RPG, Action, Adventure, Shooter, Strategy, Tactics, Platformer, Puzzle, Fighting, Racing, Sports, Simulation, Card Game, Rhythm, Visual Novel, Indie.
- How it plays: Turn-based, Real-time, Open World, Linear, Roguelike, Metroidvania, Soulslike, Deckbuilder, Survival, Stealth, Sandbox, Co-op, Multiplayer, Hack and Slash, Point and Click, Grinding.
- How it feels: Story-rich, Mystery, Detective, Psychological, Horror, Fantasy, Sci-fi, Anime, Historical, Slice of Life, Dating Sim, Life Sim, Comedy, Dark, Cozy, Emotional.
- No cap on toggles (the RAWG pull caps the merged list at 8, `mergeTags` 7870). Steam tags (`g.stTags`, `g.stG`) are NOT shown or edited here; `stApply` prepends Steam genres into `g.genres` on its own (20108): keep `g.stG` untouched.

**Notes.** "What I can learn from it" (`g.learn`, textarea, placeholder "Psychology, themes, ideas worth taking away"), "How to play it well" (`g.tips`, placeholder "Guide open, don't miss social links, play blind…").

## 3. Scores tab

**Hype** (`#e-hype`): chips 1-10 -> `g.hype` (number; none pressed when unset; no way to unset).

**Critics.** `#e-critic` number 0-100 -> `g.critic` (0 = none); under it the **"Pull score, date, tags and blurb"** button (`#e-fetch`, see §6).

**My rating.** Chips Simple | Advanced (`#e-ratemode`) -> **`S.rateMode`** `"simple"|"adv"` written at once with `save()` (**[W]**, a global setting, not per game; it is NOT part of Save/Cancel). Note under it: "Simple scores each category straight. Advanced scores the parts and works the category out from them — the number it lands on is the one everything else uses, so you can move between the two whenever you like."
- Simple: 8 sliders (0-10, step 1, "—" at 0) in this order: Story, Music, Gameplay, Replayability, Visuals, World, Characters, Pacing -> `g.mine[k]` (`story music gameplay replay visuals world chars pace`).
- Advanced: per category a header "Story **7.3**" (the average, or the simple value, or "—") and 3 sliders (0-10 step 1) -> `g.subs["cat:sub"]` (`SUBRATE`, 3764: e.g. story: writing "The writing", plot "Where it goes", ending "How it lands"; all 24 label pairs are in that table, copy it). Moving a part sets `g.mine[cat] = subAvg` (mean of the non-zero parts, 1 decimal) and moves the simple slider to its rounded value; all zero parts = leaves `mine[cat]` as it was. `subs` saved only if non-empty, else the old `g.subs` kept.
- Scored = value > 0; `avgScore` = mean of the scored categories (7371), -1 when none.

**Played** (only if `S.played` has the game's record `p`):
- "What it actually deserved": slider 0-10 step 0.5 -> `p.real` (shown one decimal, "—" if unscored, **"Unscored"** if `dk`; default 5 when unscored). Moving it clears Don't remember.
- **Don't remember** chip (`#e-dk`, drawn "unsure" icon): toggles `p.dk`. On: shows "Unscored", `real` becomes undefined; save calls `scoreWrite(rec, "dk")` -> `real 0, dk 1, no z` (13164). A real 0 is `real 0, z 1`. `Scored(p)` = not dk and number > 0 (or 0 with z).
- Note: "You hyped it N/10 before playing." (`g.hype` or "—").
- **Times through**: slider 1-12 step 1 -> `p.runs`, shown "3×".
- **When you finished it**: the month picker (`finPick`): a row "‹ 2026 ›" (‹ ›: year before/after, › disabled at the current year) and 12 month buttons Jan..Dec, future months disabled, the picked month pressed. Writes `p.t = "YYYY-MM-DD"`: same month keeps the old day, any other month uses **day 15**, the current month uses min(old day or 15, today's day). Note: "Pick the month. Your monthly and yearly recaps count the game there." Saved only if `editing.fin` matches `^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$`.
- **What got you about it**: chips grouped by 4 headings (`WHY_GROUPS`, 3559): Story and people (Characters, How they changed, Plot twists, The story, The writing, It got to me, It was funny); World and craft (The world, Finding things, Atmosphere, Art style, Music, Its ideas); Playing it (How it plays, The challenge, Out-thinking it, Getting good at it, Feeling powerful, Beating other people, Mayhem, Making things in it, Clearing it out); Around it (Who I played it with, Nostalgia). Multi-select, none allowed -> `g.why` (array of keys: chars arcs twist story write feels funny world explore mood style music ideas play hard think master power comp mayhem build finish people nost). Intro note text is long (5640) and ends with a line "N of M games tagged so far. At M, Taste starts telling you…" while fewer than `analysisNeed()` (default 3) games are tagged, plus a `relationship(g, p)` line "**title** — text" when one applies.
- **Want another run at it**: chips "Yes — Replay list" / "No" -> `p.again` (bool).
- **Move back to the queue** (`#e-unplay`): **immediately, no confirm, no Save needed**: `S.played` loses the record, `save()`, sheet closes, `render()`, `goTab("queue")`, toast "<title> is back in the queue" + sound `add` **[W]**. Note it does not change `g.state` (the next open repairs nothing because the record is gone; the game just reads as unplayed). Same for **Remove**.
