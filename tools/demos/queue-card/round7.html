Three new fronts for the open card. All three: no round stamp, the colour comes from the game's own cover (tap another game in the list to see it change), and the logo always sits in the same bottom-right spot. Still no buttons on the front: tap the cover (or in A the logo) for the game page, tap anywhere else to turn the card.

Colour field

The game's colour flows in from the left like ink and holds the info as a magazine sidebar: the status in big type with a small straight cover, then release, length, platform and genre on thin lines. The right side of the picture stays clean.
