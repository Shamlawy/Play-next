Sweep

The slab you liked, now in the game's colour: it sweeps in with a whoosh, the box art drops onto it with a soft thock, a light glints across the slab, and a ticket tears in bottom-left with the date and hours (a paper zip, then a little chime as the logo lands). Turning the card makes a card-snap.

B

Stack

Three stripes of the game's colour shuffle in like a deck to a rising chiptune arpeggio. The box art turns to face you and a holo sheen runs over it. Bottom-left, the big number rolls like a counter with ticking that slows down and ends on a ding. The card flips with a little spring.

C

Ink

The game's colour blooms out from behind the box art like a drop of ink (a water-drop bloop and a soft chord), a ripple runs out, and the box art rises through it. Bottom-left, your evenings fill in one by one, each with a plucked note. The card turns over like a page.
