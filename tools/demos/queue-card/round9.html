Taps

Box art: the game page. One tap anywhere else: the card closes into its row (tap the row to open it again). Two quick taps: the card turns over; two more turn it back. One tap on the back closes it too.

The strap

Your queue spot with a status dot, one plain line about the game (Ready to play, Out in 34 days, 23 h left, Played twice), then the platform logos and genres. Always in the game's own colour.

Sounds

Made in the app itself (no sound files), quiet, and they follow Settings › Sounds like every other sound in Play next.
